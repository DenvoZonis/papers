## 题目3

"人生天地之间，若白驹之过隙，忽然而已。"这句话道出了时间的流逝之快，让人不由得感叹生命的短暂与珍贵。我们在有限的时间里，如何才能活出无限的价值，这是每一个时代的人都在思考的问题。

### 翻译

"Life between heaven and earth is like a white colt flashing past a gap — suddenly it is gone." This saying reveals the swift passage of time, compelling one to lament the brevity and preciousness of life. In our limited time, how can we live out infinite value? This is a question that people of every era have pondered.

### 评价

- 原文表达了时间流逝之快以及生命短暂与珍贵的主题，主题明确且深刻。
- 翻译整体较为准确，但存在以下问题：
  - "white colt" 应改为 "white horse" 更符合中文原文的比喻用法。
  - "flashing past a gap" 可以更自然地表达为 "passing through a crack" 或 "flash by".
  - "compelling one to lament" 这一部分略显生硬，可以调整为 "leads one to reflect on".
- 总体来说，翻译较为准确，但用词和句式上可以稍作优化以更自然流畅。

## 题目4

本协议自双方签字之日起生效，有效期为五年。协议期满后，如双方无异议，可自动续期一年。除非经双方书面协商一致，任何一方不得单方面解除或终止本协议。

### 翻译

This Agreement shall come into force on the date of signature by both parties and shall remain valid for five years. Upon expiration of the agreement term, if both parties have no objection, it may be automatically renewed for one year. Unless otherwise agreed in writing by both parties through mutual consultation, neither party shall unilaterally terminate or cancel this Agreement.

### 评价

翻译准确且专业，使用了恰当的法律术语。结构清晰，语法正确。唯一建议的是稍作简化，例如将 "through mutual consultation" 删除，并将 "cancel" 替换为更合适的词汇。

改进建议：
- 建议删除 "through mutual consultation"。
- 建议将 "neither party shall unilaterally terminate or cancel this Agreement" 改为 "either party may not terminate or cancel this Agreement unless otherwise agreed in writing."

最终翻译：

This Agreement shall come into force on the date of signature by both parties and shall remain valid for five years. Upon expiration of the agreement term, if both parties have no objection, it may be automatically renewed for one year. Unless otherwise agreed in writing, neither party shall unilaterally terminate or cancel this Agreement.

或：

This Agreement shall come into force on the date of signature by both parties and shall remain valid for five years. Upon expiration of the agreement term, if both parties have no objection, it may be automatically renewed for one year. Unless otherwise agreed in writing, either party may not terminate or cancel this Agreement.

## 题目14
Chinese calligraphy, known as "shūfǎ" in Mandarin, is an art form that has been cultivated for over two thousand years. It is not merely a means of communication but also a reflection of the calligrapher's personality, emotions, and spiritual state. The practice requires patience, discipline, and a deep understanding of Chinese aesthetics.

### 翻译

汉字书法在普通话中称为“书法”，是一种已有两千多年历史的艺术形式。它不仅仅是一种交流手段，更是书法家个性、情感与精神状态的反映。这种实践需要耐心、纪律以及对中国美学的深刻理解。

### 评价

题目和翻译整体较为准确，但存在部分用词不够自然的问题。例如：

1. "over two thousand years" 翻译为 "已有两千多年历史" 是正确的，但可以更自然地说成 "两千多年的历史"。

2. 原文中 "a reflection of the calligrapher's personality, emotions, and spiritual state" 翻译为 "书法家个性、情感与精神状态的反映"，缺少了连接词 "and"，导致句子结构略显生硬。

3. 最后一句 "the practice requires patience, discipline, and a deep understanding of Chinese aesthetics." 翻译为 "这种实践需要耐心、纪律以及对中国美学的深刻理解。" 这里用 "以及" 作为并列连词是不够准确的，应该使用顿号分隔，即 "耐心、纪律与对...的深刻理解。"
